# Portova operating model

November 1, 2026. Prices are initial experiments, not validated willingness to pay or revenue forecasts.

Portova serves small Portaldot project teams that need a maintained launch and operations workspace. Agencies managing several projects are a later segment. The first sales offer is assisted launch configuration with a written scope and separately quoted fee. The client approves all wallet operations. Portova does not hold keys or promise launch success, fundraising, liquidity or returns.

The cloud API enforces owned-project limits in the database. Existing projects remain accessible after expiry; paid activity and invitations require an active subscription. Shared access is a workspace permission, not chain authority. A transfer offer requires both the current owner's authorization and recipient acceptance; collaborators are cleared on acceptance. Cancellation and invoices use the provider's hosted portal. Paid access depends on verified provider status and period end, not a success URL or browser flag.

Subscription revenue is separate from network fees, reserved deposits, supplied liquidity and customer wallet balances. There is no Portova token or per-swap fee in this release. Assisted setup is inquiry-led, with no automatic charge. The support inbox stores pilot, abuse, deletion and support requests for operator handling.

## Validation before scaling

Interview ten qualified teams about a recent launch and ongoing operations. Recruit three explicitly scoped pilots; ask for payment before building bespoke features. Track activation (saved project with a verified object), time to useful result, independent weekly return usage, willingness to renew, support minutes per account and variable infrastructure costs. These are experimental targets, not proof of demand.

Do not mistake wallet connections, subsidized transactions, wash volume or hackathon awards for paying retention. If customers launch once and do not return, price episodic services rather than forcing subscriptions. Establish a repeatable customer channel before expanding chains or building a marketplace.

## Commercial activation gate

Live checkout stays disabled until operator-approved commercial terms, jurisdiction/provider eligibility, taxes, cancellation/refund handling, privacy disclosures and support ownership are settled. Obtain scope-specific legal review for any real-value token-sale, exchange or transfer service; a noncustodial design does not resolve classification by itself. The service-information page is a product disclosure and requires operator legal review before paid launch.

No paid customer, revenue, distribution agreement or institutional ecosystem buyer is claimed by this implementation.
