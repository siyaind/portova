## Final material

- Team-authored name, members and one-sentence overview.
- Public repository, runnable final source, setup/deployment instructions and documentation.
- Runnable demo and a publicly accessible video of no more than three minutes, demonstrating core functionality and Portaldot usage.
- Pitch deck PDF or public link, covering problem, Portaldot relevance, functionality evidence and continued support.
- Contract address and transaction hashes where applicable. Portova uses runtime pallets, so no custom contract is claimed.
- At least three valid event-period commits; existing history contains more than three. Maintain development records and avoid landing all core modules in the final 24 hours.
- Review dependency/asset attribution and final code-freeze requirements.

The technical package supplies source facts, evidence and a walkthrough. The team must finalize its own narrative/deck, publish a final video link and submit/update the entry. This task does not certify eligibility or submit event forms.
